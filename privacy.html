Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who uses this app

gcal-agent is used by one person: its owner. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads and writes events in the owner's Google Calendar. It also reads the list of the owner's calendars.

What it stores

A login token is stored on the owner's private server. A log of recent changes is kept there too so that a change can be undone. Nothing else is stored.

Sharing

Calendar data is not sold or shared with anyone. It is not used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
